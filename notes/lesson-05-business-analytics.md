# Business Analytics

Related fields: Data Analytics, Data Science, Data Engineering.

## What is Business Analytics?
- Transforming raw data into actionable insights.
- Analyze business performance and product performance.
- To drive business decisions using data to better plan for the future.
- We want to drive business growth (business profitability).
- Giving solutions to business problems.

### Understand the Business
- **Understand the business model:** understanding how the business makes money.
- **Domain expertise:** in-depth knowledge and understanding of a specific industry.
- **Identify the problem:** driven by data.

### Consumers vs Producers

| Consumers | Producers |
|---|---|
| They move with trends | Owners / Investors |
| What consumers are saying | Business model (stable) |
|  | What is required to run the business |

- **CSAT (Customer Satisfaction Score):** measures how satisfied customers are.
- **How we measure success:** KPI (Key Performance Indicator).

## Launching a Product

**Quantitative KPIs**
- Bring new customers
- Sell more
- See diversity
- Increase revenue
- Increase profit
- Remain competitive
- Brand visibility

**Qualitative KPIs**
- Brand visibility
- Revenue = Price × Quantity (`R = P x Qty`)

### Cost
- **Variable Cost** + **Fixed Cost** = **Total Cost**
- Total cost is linked to the products we sell in the business.
- The goal of a business analyst is to reduce costs.

### Key Metrics to Track
- Number of sales/transactions — should always be increasing.
- Number of units sold — increase target (e.g. 1000+).
- Number of customers — increase target (e.g. 10+).

### Profit
```
Profit = Revenue (↑) − Cost (↓)
P = R − C
```
Revenue must be high and cost must be low. (Check what is costing you too much.)

### Campaigns
- Awareness
- Attract customers
- Introduce a new product
- Stimulate customers (to act in the direction of purchasing your product)
- Business analytics informs what kind of campaign should be run.

## Post-Implementation Reviews (PIR)
**Post-campaign analysis** — evaluating a marketing campaign after it has ended.

| KPI | Target (Pre) | Target (Post) | Control (Pre) | Control (Post) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue / Profit Margin | 10k | 25k | 5k | 6k |
| Frequency / ARPU | 108 | 220 | 80 | 85 |
| Conversion Rate | — | — | — | — |

- **ARPU:** Average Revenue Per Objective/User.
- Target group: "We tell you what to do."
- Control group: "We don't tell you what to do."

We do these post-implementation reviews to answer:
- "Did the campaign achieve its targets, and what can we learn from it?"
- We analyze the campaign objectives: "What was the campaign supposed to achieve?"
- KPIs measure the campaign's performance.
